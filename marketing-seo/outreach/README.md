# outreach/

Sequences for inbound and partnership conversations. **No scraped lists, no purchased contact data** —
that would violate the privacy promises the product makes on its own site.

## Inbound (demo request)

| Step | Timing | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | within 2 working days | reply referencing the specifics they wrote (squad, data, question) |
| 2 | +3 days if no reply | one short follow-up with a concrete next step; then stop |
| 3 | after the call | written summary: what we can do, what we cannot do, what we need from them |

## Outbound (partnership)

Only to organisations where a specific, factual reason exists ("you publish match data we could
integrate with"). One message, no sequence. Record the basis for contact — consent or legitimate
interest — because the privacy policy says we do not buy lists.

## What we never do
* Fabricate a customer logo or a case study.
* Claim accuracy figures not measured on our own forecasts.
* Send a cold sequence to an individual coach's personal address.
