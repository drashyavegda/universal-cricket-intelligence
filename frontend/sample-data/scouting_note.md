# Scouting note — trial block, Ahmedabad (synthetic example)

This note exercises the free-text / PDF ingestion path. All names are fictional.

## Observation
The left-arm spinner from the Sabarmati Strikers trial group bowled six overs in the
nets with a repeatable release, but his stock ball drifted into the right-hander's pads
under fatigue. Economy in the two trial matches was respectable, yet the sample is six
overs — not enough to rank him on economy alone.

## Interpretation
Treat early-trial economy as a signal, not a rating. Where the cohort sample is thin the
platform should widen confidence intervals and reduce the score rather than inflate it.

## Recommended next step
Collect three more matches of charted data before the auction, and record wicket-taking
balls separately from dot balls. Do not impute the missing catches data.
