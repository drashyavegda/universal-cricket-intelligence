## Truthfulness rule

The public pages must not claim compliance with anything not actually implemented (no "GDPR certified",
no "ISO 27001", no "SOC 2"). The current privacy page states plainly what is collected, that analytics
are consent-gated, and how to contact the operator — nothing more.
