# Annual retrospective — report definition

A season-level review. Deliberately heavier on what the platform *could not* tell you than on successes.

## Sections
1. Season in numbers — matches, players, data volume, coverage of the squad.
2. Model performance — forecasts made vs outcomes (Brier, log loss), with sample sizes.
3. Selection outcomes — shortlisted players who were picked, and their subsequent performance band.
4. Data quality trend — quality score by month, and the worst three imports with cause.
5. Cost — AI spend, hosting, and cost per generated report.
6. Gaps — features that were promised but not delivered, stated plainly.
7. Next season — three changes, each with a measurable success criterion.

## Rule
Any number that cannot be recomputed from stored data is removed from this report, not estimated.
